How to Rank in the Google Maps 3-Pack in 2026

Relevance, distance and prominence decide who shows up. Here is what you can actually influence.

The three ranking factors

Google says local results are based mainly on relevance, distance and prominence. You cannot change where a customer is standing, but you can improve how well your profile matches what they search and how well known your business is online.

Start with your categories

Your primary category is the strongest relevance signal you control. Check what the top three competitors use and choose the most specific option that honestly describes you.

Build prominence slowly

Reviews, mentions on trusted local sites and consistent listings all add up. Avoid shortcuts: purchased reviews and fake addresses are the quickest route to a suspension.
