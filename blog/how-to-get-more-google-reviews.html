A Simple System to Get More Google Reviews

You do not need tricks. You need to ask at the right moment, every time.

Ask right after a good experience

The best time is when the customer is happiest: after the job is done or the meal is finished. A short, friendly request works far better than an email a week later.

Make it one tap

Create your direct review link and turn it into a QR code for the counter, receipts or invoices. Fewer steps means more reviews.

Reply to everyone

A calm, specific reply to a bad review shows future customers how you handle problems. It often matters more than the rating itself.
